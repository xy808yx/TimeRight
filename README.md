# Time Right

A personalized **circadian-rhythm planner** — light, caffeine, movement, and wind-down timed to your real day.

**Live:** https://xy808yx.github.io/TimeRight/

- Single-file PWA (`index.html`) — no build step, no account, saves locally in your browser.
- A short 10-step consultation builds your day: morning light window, caffeine cut-off, evening dim-down, and a consistent lights-out target.
- **Today** view with a live sun-arc, **Plan** view ranking your timing bottlenecks, and a dark **Science** view with citations.

Grounded in published circadian research and the protocols of Andrew Huberman, Ph.D. and Dr. Abud Bakri, MD. Educational wellness only — not medical advice.
